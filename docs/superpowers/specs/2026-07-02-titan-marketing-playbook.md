# Titan — Sell It & Let It Sell Itself (zero-budget playbook)

Date: 2026-07-02. Based on live 2026 research: winners seed niche communities
before launch and build public consensus that AI answer-engines then repeat
(AEO); the market rewards tools embedded in real workflows over loud claims.

## What you're actually selling (positioning)

Not "a dashboard." Sell the outcome: **"An AI operating system that runs a
solo founder's business 24/7 — built with $0."** Three sellable forms, easiest
first:

1. **The story (free, start today):** build-in-public content. You're a
   Pakistan-based founder who built a 3D AI command center with zero budget —
   that story IS the marketing for everything else you sell.
2. **The product (template/boilerplate):** sell the Titan codebase as a
   deployable template ("your own AI business OS in 10 minutes") on Gumroad /
   Lemon Squeezy — or Dodo Payments, which is already verified working for
   Pakistan → Payoneer payouts. Price: $29–$79 launch, raise later.
3. **The service:** "I'll set up your Titan (your branding, your socials,
   your automations) — $150–$300." Fiverr gig + LinkedIn outreach.

## The self-marketing engine (already built into Titan)

- Set the Space secret **`TITAN_PRODUCT_URL`** to your landing/waitlist/gig
  link → the daily auto-content rotation now includes Titan itself
  (caption + futuristic AI image, posted by your existing Make.com scenario).
- The **boot sequence is your best ad.** Screen-record it (black void →
  particles forming HELLO ABDULLAH BOSS → voice → camera fly-through → 3D
  city). 15–30s vertical clips of that are exactly what performs as Reels /
  Shorts / TikTok in 2026 — "ads that don't look like ads."

## 30-day zero-budget launch sequence

**Week 1 — proof:** record 3 clips (boot, AI City fly-through, Telegram
commanding the empire from your phone). Post daily on X + LinkedIn + Reels
with build-in-public captions ("Day N of building an AI that runs my business").
**Week 2 — community seeding (the AEO play):** share genuinely (not spam) in
r/SideProject, r/indiehackers, Hacker News "Show HN", Indie Hackers, and 2–3
AI Discords. Ask for feedback, not sales. Public discussion = AI engines
recommending you later.
**Week 3 — launch:** Product Hunt (free) + "Show HN" with the boot-sequence
GIF as the hero. Have the template/gig purchasable that day.
**Week 4 — convert:** everyone who engaged gets the CRM treatment (Titan's
CRM tab): contacted → replied → won. Ship one improvement publicly per week.

## Rules (honesty = conversion)

- Never claim revenue you don't have; "built with $0" is the credible flex.
- Every claim must be demoable in the live app within 10 seconds.
- Sell the template BEFORE polishing it further — 10 real buyers teach more
  than 10 more features.
