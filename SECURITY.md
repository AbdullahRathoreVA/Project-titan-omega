# Security

If you find a vulnerability, please email **rathoreabdullah816@gmail.com**
instead of opening a public issue.

Helpful to include:

- what you found and where (URL or file)
- steps to reproduce
- what an attacker could do with it

Please only test against your own account. Do not try to read or change
another customer's data, and do not run load tests against
titanomega-ai.com.

Things that are in scope and worth reporting: one account reaching another
account's data, anything readable without signing in that should not be, and
anything that spends the platform's AI or search quota without a signed-in
account.
